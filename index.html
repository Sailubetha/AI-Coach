CATEGORIES & DIVISIONS

Divisions of Non-Verbal Body Language

1. Posture & Spinal Position

  • Sit Upright: Keep shoulders square and chest open to display confidence.
  • Avoid Slouching: Hunching forward over the camera conveys fatigue or lack of interest.

2. Eye Contact & Facial Expressions

  • Direct Camera Gaze: Look into the webcam lens while speaking (60-70% eye contact).
  • Avoid Looking Down: Frequently averting gaze signals nervousness or uncertainty.

3. Gestures & Hand Control

  • Controlled Gestures: Use smooth, natural hand gestures within camera frame to emphasize points.
  • Stop Fidgeting: Avoid rapid hand movements, hair touching, finger tapping, or seat restlessness.

4. Vocal Pace & Filler Words

  • Steady Speed: Maintain a clear pace of 120–150 words per minute.
  • Eliminate Fillers: Reduce words like "um", "uh", "like", and "basically".
